This tool was born inside a Reddit-story video generator.

Every word gets its own highlight as it is spoken.

The active word flashes yellow and scales up to one hundred fifteen percent.

Short words are paired up. Longer words get a line all to themselves.

You can feed it an SRT file, a plain text script, or a raw audio file.

Whisper handles the word-level timing automatically.

The style is fully configurable. Font size, colors, position, outline, shadow.

Burn subtitles into any video you want. Vertical. Horizontal. Anything.
